## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Logistics Analysis Dashboard** developed using Microsoft Power BI to track shipments, monitor fleet status, optimize routes, and ensure on-time delivery performance across global regions.

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of total shipments, on-time delivery percentage, average delivery time, total distance covered, logistics cost, active customers, fleet utilization, and shipment-level delivery statuses[cite: 12, 13, 14, 15, 16].

It is designed to simulate a real-world supply chain and logistics control-tower environment, helping operations teams and leadership monitor monthly shipment volume, identify delivery delays or exceptions, and evaluate regional and transport-mode costs[cite: 12, 13, 14, 15, 16].

The solution brings executive KPIs, shipment logs, fleet and route analytics, SLA performance, and individual shipment drillthroughs into a centralized interactive reporting system[cite: 12, 13, 14, 15, 16].

## 📈 Dashboard Features & Business Analysis

### 1️⃣ Overview & Shipment Trends

Provides a high-level control-tower view of shipment activity, transport modes, and regional distribution[cite: 12].

**Key Analysis:**
- Total Shipments (1,800), On-Time Delivery % (89.9%), Avg Delivery Time (4.6 Days), Total Distance (3M KM), Total Logistics Cost (₹5.08M), and Total Customers (968).
- Monthly Shipment Trend and On-Time Delivery % from Jan through Dec.
- Shipment breakdown by Transport Mode: Road (58.5%), Sea (22.11%), Air (12.56%), and Rail (6.83%).
- Product volume by category (led by Sports at 446 and Electronics at 444), regional delivery status (Delayed, Delivered, Exception), and recent shipments feed[cite: 12].

### 2️⃣ Shipment Details & Destination Cost Analysis

Analyzes individual shipments, delivery outcomes, and logistics spending across global destination cities[cite: 13].

**Key Analysis:**
- Summary cards for Total Shipment (1,800), Avg Delivery Time (4.6 Days), Delivered Shipment (1,618), and Delayed Shipment (104).
- Monthly shipment volume and on-time delivery performance trend.
- Total Logistics Cost by Destination City led by Shanghai (₹0.52M), Singapore (₹0.44M), Mumbai (₹0.44M), and Dubai (₹0.41M).
- Detailed Shipment Details table with Shipment ID, Shipment Date, City, Region, Shipment Type, Mode, Delivery Status, Expected Delivery Date, and Quantity[cite: 13].

### 3️⃣ Fleet & Route Analysis

Monitors vehicle availability, utilization efficiency, distance coverage, and regional logistics costs[cite: 14].

**Key Analysis:**
- Fleet summary tracking Total Vehicles (120), Fleet Utilization % (68.7%), Total Distance (2,994,095 KM), and Total Logistics Cost (₹5,081.71K).
- Vehicles by Fleet Status: In Transit (71.67%), Idle (15%), Maintenance (10%), and Out of Service (3.33%).
- Logistics Cost and Total Distance by Region led by Asia (₹2,164.08K / 1,286,594 KM) and North America (₹1,341.23K / 775,146 KM).
- Vehicle Details table displaying Vehicle ID, Vehicle Type, Transport Mode, Region, Fleet Status, Utilization %, Last Service Date, and Capacity KG[cite: 14].

### 4️⃣ Delivery Performance & Shipment Drillthrough

Evaluates SLA reliability across modes and regions while enabling single-shipment root-cause investigation[cite: 15, 16].

**Key Analysis:**
- Delivery SLA KPIs tracking On-Time Delivery % (89.9%), Avg Delivery Time (4.6 Days), Delayed Shipments (104), and Exception Shipments (78).
- On-Time Delivery % by Transport Mode led by Air (93.4%) and Road (90.4%), ahead of Rail (89.4%) and Sea (86.7%).
- On-Time Delivery % by Region led by Asia (91.3%) and Europe (90.0%), compared to South America (89.8%), North America (88.2%), and Africa (86.1%).
- Delayed Shipment Table and Drillthrough view (e.g., `TRK-01228` showing May 31, 2024 shipment date, June 3, 2024 expected delivery vs. June 4, 2024 actual delivery — 1 Day Late, Quantity 1, Revenue 6,107.00, Distance 1,043 KM)[cite: 15, 16].

## 📊 Visualizations Used

The dashboard incorporates the following Power BI visuals:

- KPI Cards — Shipments, on-time delivery %, delivery time, distance, logistics cost, customers, vehicles, and fleet utilization monitoring.
- Combo & Line Charts — Monthly shipment volume and on-time delivery % trends[cite: 12, 13, 15].
- Doughnut Charts — Transport mode share, vehicle fleet status, and shipment delivery status breakdowns[cite: 12, 13, 14, 15].
- Horizontal & Column Bar Charts — Product shipment volume, logistics cost by city and region, distance by region, and on-time % by mode and region[cite: 12, 13, 14, 15].
- Map Visual — Regional shipment distribution across global regions[cite: 12].
- Detailed & Drillthrough Tables — Recent shipments, full shipment logs, vehicle fleet records, delayed shipments, and single-shipment drillthrough views[cite: 12, 13, 14, 15, 16].
- Navigation Buttons & Slicers — Page navigation buttons plus `Month Name`, `Region`, `Shipment Type`, `Transport Mode`, `Delivery Status`, `Fleet Status`, and `Vehicle Type` filters[cite: 12, 13, 14, 15].

## 🎛️ Interactive Dashboard Filters

The dashboard includes multiple filtering options for targeted logistics and fleet analysis:

| Filter | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Month Name | Analyze monthly shipment volume, costs, and on-time trends[cite: 12, 13, 14, 15] |
| Region | Compare performance across Asia, North America, Europe, South America, and Africa[cite: 12, 13, 14, 15] |
| Shipment Type | Filter across Economy, Standard, and Express shipment categories[cite: 12, 13] |
| Transport Mode | Evaluate Road, Sea, Air, and Rail performance[cite: 12, 13, 14, 15] |
| Delivery Status | Isolate Delivered, Delayed, or Exception shipments[cite: 13, 15] |
| Fleet Status | Filter vehicles by In Transit, Idle, Maintenance, or Out of Service[cite: 14, 15] |
| Vehicle Type | Analyze fleet metrics by vehicle type[cite: 14] |
