## 📊 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Bank of Baroda Executive Banking Dashboard**, developed using Microsoft Power BI to monitor financial performance, customer growth, lending activities, deposits, profitability, and regional business performance.

The dashboard transforms banking data into meaningful business insights through interactive visualizations, KPI cards, financial trends, customer segmentation, and geographic analysis.

It is designed to provide management with a consolidated view of banking operations and support data-driven strategic decision-making.

### 🎯 Business Objectives

- Monitor overall banking revenue, deposits, loans, and profitability.
- Analyze customer growth and customer segmentation.
- Evaluate loan portfolio composition and credit risk.
- Track Gross NPA and financial performance indicators.
- Compare business performance across states and regions.
- Identify growth opportunities and areas requiring management attention.

## 📊 Dashboard Features & Analysis

### 1️⃣ Executive Financial Performance

Provides a consolidated overview of the bank's financial health.

**Key visualizations:**
- Total Revenue and Net Profit trends.
- Revenue growth compared with the previous year.
- Total Deposits and Loans.
- Gross NPA performance.
- ROA and ROE indicators.
- Year-over-year financial comparisons.

### 2️⃣ Customer Analytics — Customer 360

Focuses on understanding customer growth and customer composition.

**Key analysis:**
- Existing vs. new customer growth.
- Customer segmentation.
- Customer acquisition trends.
- Customer churn monitoring.
- Customer value analysis.

### 3️⃣ Loan & Credit Risk Analysis

Evaluates the bank's lending portfolio and credit quality.

**Key analysis:**
- Loan portfolio composition.
- Agriculture, Corporate, Education, Infrastructure, MSME, and Retail lending.
- Gross NPA monitoring.
- Credit risk indicators.
- Loan portfolio performance.

### 4️⃣ Branch & Regional Performance

Provides geographic insights into banking business performance.

**Key analysis:**
- State-wise deposits.
- Regional business contribution.
- Top-performing states.
- Geographic performance comparison.
- Year-over-year regional growth.

### 5️⃣ Customer Churn Analysis

Focuses on customer retention and customer relationship performance.

**Key analysis:**
- Customer churn monitoring.
- Customer retention opportunities.
- Customer segmentation.
- Customer behavior analysis.

## 📉 Visualizations Used

The dashboard combines multiple Power BI visualizations to make banking performance easier to understand.

- KPI Cards — Financial performance indicators.
- Clustered Column Chart — Revenue and profit trends.
- Doughnut Charts — Business mix and loan composition.
- Stacked Column Chart — Customer growth.
- Filled Map — Geographic banking performance.
- Bar Charts — State-wise and category-wise comparisons.
- Tables — Regional deposits and growth analysis.
- Slicers — Financial year and state selection.
