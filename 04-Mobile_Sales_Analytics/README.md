## 📖 About the Project

This project is an **End-to-End sales, product, and customer analytics dashboard** built for **Mobile Sales Analytics**, covering multi-year retail performance across India.

The dashboard consolidates transactional sales, product catalog, regional distribution, and customer demographic data into **4 interactive pages** (including a custom drill-through view) that give leadership a single source of truth for tracking revenue growth, brand performance, channel efficiency, and customer satisfaction.

**Business questions this dashboard answers:**
- How are total revenue, units sold, and average selling price trending month over month and year over year?
- Which smartphone brands and models generate the highest revenue vs. highest unit volume?
- Which sales channels (Marketplace, Online, Dealer, Retail Store) and states drive the strongest sales?
- What is our customer demographic profile (age, gender), payment preference, and rating distribution?
